GBM atlas — cohorts, papers and sources

Harmonized human IDH-wildtype glioblastoma snATAC-seq atlas (v2026-11-15). One row per cohort. Every URL to re-download the raw data, verify the citation, and confirm the per-patient inclusion decisions.
Patients = unique individuals (biological replicates), the primary replication unit in all atlas scoring. YES rows were pseudobulked into the atlas; NO rows were excluded for the reason stated.
Libraries = sequencing libraries. Hidden by default (toggle below). No GBM cohort in the atlas is genetically multiplexed, so 1 library = 1 patient for every row. Multi-sample patients (de Jong multi-region, Wang 2024 primary/recurrent, Pai Core/Margin, Terekhanova CPTAC multi-section) are collapsed to a single patient id in the per-cell-type pseudobulk.
Donor-level metadata file points to the row in cohort_attribution.csv where per-patient inclusion rationale lives.
