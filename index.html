Reports

  • GBM snATAC enhancer atlas — top-1000 per cell type
    We combined all publicly available data on the locations of the DNA switches that are active in each cell type inside glioblastoma brain tumors. This identifies sequences that may be helpful in selectively targeting certain cells in and around these tumors.
    67 patients × 10 cohorts × 1.65 M nuclei × 2.09 M union peaks. 14 cell-type tabs (10 primary + 4 malignant subtypes) with per-patient, per cell-type resolution. Peaks scored for strength (fragments/cell) and specificity (log2fc over next most open cell type).
    bioRxiv: pending approval
    Metadata: Cohorts, publications, and data sources (metadata.csv, all cols)
    Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23252323
    Full v1.2 deposit — AWS Registry of Open Data
    gbm_atlas_v1.2_integrated.h5ad — 34.8 GB · per-cell integrated atlas (1,585,983 nuclei × 1,958,753 peaks, sparse CSR, obs/var/uns stamped with atlas provenance)
    per_cohort/<slug>/<slug>_at_v5.h5ad × 10 — 94.5 GB total · per-cohort per-cell h5ads (Pai + Terekhanova on 2.09M-peak axis; eight v1.1 cohorts on 1.96M-peak v1.1 axis)
    Plus pseudobulk matrix, peak reference, per-cell-type top-1000 tables, scoring scripts, METHODS/SCHEMA/CHANGELOG. 131 GB total, 101 objects.
    S3 (no-sign-request, us-east-1): s3://mcginnislab-gbm-atlas/  ·  HTTPS: mcginnislab-gbm-atlas.s3.amazonaws.com

  • Under constructionNormal brain snATAC enhancer atlas — top-1000 per cell type (v3)
    We combined all publicly available data on the locations of the DNA switches that are active in each cell type inside the normal human brain. This identifies sequences that may be helpful in selectively targeting certain cells for various kinds of brain problems. Methods & peak set →
    Up to 830 donors × 25 cohorts in abundant cell types (OPC 830 / Oligo 745 / Inhibitory 704 / Astrocyte 704 / Microglia 661 / Excitatory 528 / Endothelial 362); rare types (Meninges, T_cell, Serotonergic, Dopaminergic) have single-digit to ~20 donors. 41 M barcodes (6 M post nnz≥1k QC) × 990 K union peaks. 16 major cell-type tabs with per-cohort replication and surgical/postmortem split, Design B narrow sub-peaks (median ~518 bp) rendered inline for AAV cloning, direct UCSC Genome Browser deep links.

Published-paper data (GEO)

  • GSE292722 — AAV transduction of ex vivo human brain tissue
    We tested which of today's commonly used gene-therapy delivery vehicles actually reach which cell types in living human brain tissue, so researchers can choose the right vehicle for the cells they're trying to treat.
    10X 3′ snRNA-seq across day-0 flash-frozen, day-14 cultured untreated, and day-14 cultured with AAV vector library. Adult and pediatric donors.
    Paper: McGinnis et al., Mol Ther Methods Clin Dev 2025;33(2):101494.
    Public 2025-06-01
  • GSE328077 — Cell-type identity in ex vivo human brain tissue
    We showed that human brain tissue kept alive in the lab for two weeks keeps the identity of its cell types intact, which lets us test new ideas and therapies on real human brain tissue before trying them in patients.
    snRNA-seq of six patient brain samples (2 pediatric temporal lobectomies, 1 adult frontal cortex, 2 glioblastomas, 1 medulloblastoma) at day 0 (flash-frozen) vs day 14 (organotypic slice culture); 12 libraries total. Processed counts only; raw FASTQs withheld for patient privacy.
    Paper: McGinnis et al., Brain Communications 2026;8(2):fcag073.
    Public 2026-04-16
